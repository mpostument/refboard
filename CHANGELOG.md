# What's new

## 1.2.0

- **My set** - mark what you own from the whole Copic range (357) and the Faber-Castell pencil range (116), keep several
  named sets, and save a set to a file or load it back. Match a palette to your set, with up to three colours worth getting
  next. Paint sets: pick tubes and save them as a palette the Colour studio, eyedropper and Palettes use.
- **Tags** - your own tags on pictures (`#` on the grid, `T` in a session or the grid); tag chips filter the grid.
- **Draw these** - takes a number and prefers pictures you have not drawn lately, and starred ones.
- **Generate** - save your choices as a recipe under a name, or "Same, but light". A status line shows whether ComfyUI is
  ready and its queue, and re-checks by itself while it is down. A one-line summary of your set links to changing it.

## 1.1.0

- **Stars and come back to this** - press `L` to star a picture (shown as a star on the grid, with a filter beside search) and `B`
  to mark one to come back to. After a session a card shows poses, time and skipped ones, with Draw these again.
- **Palettes** - export a palette as a `.gpl` file or a PNG.
- **Generate** - after a run of two or more pictures, a strip lets you keep one (the rest are deleted) or keep all.

## 1.0.0 - the first release

Refboard does three things:

- **References** - a library of your pictures as a grid, and timed drawing sessions with the tools that help you look: value
  split, grayscale, squint, grids and guides, an angle tool, an eyedropper with paint recipes, pencil grades, and Compare, which
  lays a photo of your drawing over the reference. Museums search the Met and the Rijksmuseum.
- **Generate** - references made to order by your own ComfyUI, from choices rather than prompts.
- **Colours** - palettes with how to mix each colour from your paints, and a Colour studio with a picture's palette, a colour
  wheel, a mixing chart and a pigment guide.

And a small extra: 3D forms, light on simple solids.
