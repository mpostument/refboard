# What's new

## 1.0.0 - the first release

Refboard does three things:

- **References** - a library of your pictures as a grid, and timed drawing sessions with the tools that help you look: value
  split, grayscale, squint, grids and guides, an angle tool, an eyedropper with paint recipes, pencil grades, and Compare, which
  lays a photo of your drawing over the reference. Museums search the Met and the Rijksmuseum.
- **Generate** - references made to order by your own ComfyUI, from choices rather than prompts.
- **Colours** - palettes with how to mix each colour from your paints, and a Colour studio with a picture's palette, a colour
  wheel, a mixing chart and a pigment guide.

And a small extra: 3D forms, light on simple solids.
